The State Snack Index

Which vending machine favorite does each state search for most? We compared Google search interest for 15 of America's best-known vending snacks across all 50 states and Washington, DC.

Key takeaways

    Source: Kande VendTech analysis of Google Trends data, 15 snack brands, Nov 2025 to Oct 2026. kandevendtech.com2.7x = 2.7 times the snack's national share of searches

    America's most searched vending snacks

    Average share of searches across the 15 snacks, all states weighted equally.

    How we did it

    Kande VendTech pulled 12 months of Google Trends search interest (November 1, 2025 to October 31, 2026) for 15 snack brands commonly stocked in US vending machines: 9 salty (Doritos, Cheetos, Takis, Lay's, Pringles, Fritos, Funyuns, SunChips, Ruffles) and 6 sweet (M&M's, Reese's, Skittles, Snickers, KitKat, Twix).

    Google Trends compares at most five terms at once, so we ran four comparisons that each included Doritos as a common anchor and rescaled every snack against it. Snickers and Cheetos appeared in two comparisons as a consistency check; their rescaled values matched to within 0.05.

    • Most searched: the snack with the largest share of the state's searches across all 15.
    • Signature snack: the snack whose share in that state is highest relative to its national share (1.0x = the national average). It shows what makes a state different, rather than what is popular everywhere.

    We used Google's brand topics rather than exact search phrases, so spelling variants (for example "reeses" and "Reese's") are counted together. Search interest measures curiosity and intent, not sales. Smaller states and smaller brands carry more rounding noise, so where the top two snacks are within half a percentage point we count it as a tie.

    To test the results against Google's rounding, we re-ran the calculation 2,000 times with every figure nudged randomly within its rounding range. Where a state's signature snack changed in more than 1 in 10 runs, we show it as having no clear standout. Every state with a most searched snack kept the same winner in at least 95% of runs.
